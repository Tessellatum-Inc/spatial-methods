## Review checklist
- [ ] Beneficiary and problem are specific.
- [ ] Demand evidence is distinguished from assumptions.
- [ ] Offer, exclusions and service obligations are clear.
- [ ] Pricing has a defensible rationale.
- [ ] Acquisition and delivery paths are plausible.
- [ ] Costs, time and maintenance are accounted for.
- [ ] Unit economics are assessed beyond gross revenue.
- [ ] Rights, safety and compliance are considered.
- [ ] Reusable assets and strategic value are recorded.
- [ ] A bounded validation experiment and decision rule exist.
