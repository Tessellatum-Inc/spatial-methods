# Spatial Methods — Exploration template

**Purpose:** A reusable starting structure for curiosity-led investigations. Adapt it to the subject; do not fill every section mechanically.

> **Editorial rule:** Use the smallest structure that genuinely helps someone take the curiosity further. A short orientation or annotated resource shelf can be a complete and valuable response.

### 2. The curiosity

- **Central question:** What are we trying to understand or investigate?

### 3. Orientation
Write a clear opening that helps a newcomer understand the subject without flattening its complexity.

- What is it?
- What is it not?
- What are the important distinctions?
- What should someone understand before going deeper?
- What common misconceptions should be avoided?

## Publication note

An exploration may be published in stages. Mark what is ready, what is still being investigated and what is deliberately out of scope. Do not disguise an early map as a complete treatment.
