

## Owner clarification — 18 November 2026

The project owner has confirmed that the supplied *Spatial Analysis Algorithms: Complete Compendium* is their own project and compilation, developed with AI assistance. The earlier open question about whether the source was an unidentified third-party submission is therefore resolved for project-provenance purposes. See [compendium provenance and authorship](compendium-provenance.md).

This confirmation does **not** automatically clear every third-party passage, table, figure, dataset, code sample or other asset that may appear within the compilation or its derivative materials. Those items still require source-level review. The compendium's original project-authored material may be treated as owner-originated, but no project-wide licence is applied by this statement, and no third-party rights are overridden.
