The project owner has confirmed that the 47-page compendium is their own project and compilation, developed with AI assistance. This resolves the prior uncertainty about whether the compilation was an unidentified third-party submission. The owner statement and its scope are recorded in [compendium provenance and authorship](compendium-provenance.md).
