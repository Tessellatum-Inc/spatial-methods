# Welcome to Spatial Methods

Welcome. Spatial Methods is a place to investigate questions across spatial data, methods, technologies and computation.

You do not need to arrive with a complete research question or follow a prescribed course. Begin with a concept, a paper, a dataset, a tool, an application or a problem you want to understand.

One subject can support many kinds of work. You might want to understand an idea, study the evidence, practise a method, build an implementation, prepare teaching material or apply something to a real problem. Move between these intentions as your interests change.

## Find your way around

- **Start Here:** You are here. Use this post to understand the project and find your next step.
- **Exploration Library:** Question-led investigations that connect concepts, methods, tools, evidence and applications.
- **Curiosity Desk:** Suggest a question, resource, technology or problem worth examining.
- **Laboratory:** Practical investigations, worked examples, notebooks and reproducible workflows when they add value.
- **Research Dispatches:** Evidence-led notes on developments, research, technology, data and policy.

## How the material is organised

GitHub is the canonical home for versioned technical material, code, notebooks, references and corrections. Patreon is the member-facing experience: guided entry points, companion material, editorial context and participation.

We curate and connect strong existing resources rather than pretending to replace the people and institutions that create them. We make original material where it adds value, and we state limitations and provenance.

## A good first move

Choose one question that genuinely interests you. Follow one useful route and leave with one thing you understand better, one source worth keeping or one small thing you have tried.

You do not have to consume everything. The aim is to make your curiosity productive.
