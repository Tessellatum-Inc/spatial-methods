## 2. Email draft — KISM

**To:** info@kism.ac.ke  
**Subject:** Request to circulate independent photogrammetry review invitation

Dear Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing Department,

I am coordinating an evidence-led photogrammetry methods exercise and would like to ask whether KISM could circulate a short reviewer invitation to suitably experienced staff, alumni or professional contacts.

We are seeking an independent reviewer to assess 206 pre-existing image-point observations across 32 original UAV images. The review is focused on image-observation usability: target visibility and identity, centring, blur, occlusion and ambiguity. It is not a request to certify survey accuracy or reproduce the original bundle adjustment.

The work follows a documented role-blind protocol. Reviewers will disclose relevant prior exposure and conflicts before receiving the review materials. We aim to have two reviewers complete the work independently, preserve their original submissions and adjudicate any disagreements transparently.

We would particularly welcome someone with practical experience in aerial or close-range photogrammetry, digital image measurement, ground-control target identification or photogrammetric QA.

Would the department be willing to circulate the invitation or suggest the appropriate institutional route for doing so?

I can provide the concise invitation and technical handoff for your consideration.

Kind regards,  
Tessellatum / Spatial Methods project coordinator

## 5. Response handling

When an institution responds positively:

1. Thank them and request the candidate's short relevant-experience summary.
2. Send the disclosure questions from [reviewer-recruitment-and-intake.md](reviewer-recruitment-and-intake.md), without the proposed split or point roles.
3. Check prior exposure to the HEIG-VD dataset, original Metashape picks, OPK file, annotated derivatives, source project and any involvement in acquisition/publication.
4. Check professional/institutional relationships with the dataset publisher, project team and other reviewer.
5. Confirm availability, expected time commitment, review deadline, secure submission route and whether the reviewer can use original full-resolution images.
6. Accept only after the independence screen is reviewed. Then provide the same pinned-source handoff and role-blind materials to both reviewers.
7. Preserve independent submissions separately; do not merge or adjudicate until both reviewers have submitted.

## 6. Scope and handling safeguards

- Do not disclose proposed control/checkpoint point IDs or the split key.
- Do not ask the reviewer to use publisher Metashape pixel measurements, supplied OPK or a prior project.
- The review packet contains navigation derivatives and blank forms, not original full-resolution imagery.
- The dataset is licensed CC BY 4.0; retain HEIG-VD Geomatics attribution.
- Agree a private submission route directly with each reviewer. Never request upload to a public GitHub issue, pull request or repository path.
- Do not publish reviewer names, contact details, conflict disclosures or private submissions without permission.
- Do not promise payment, honoraria, authorship, institutional endorsement or a deadline that has not been agreed.
