# Independent Reviewer Prospecting — HEIG-VD Photogrammetry Rehearsal

**Status:** desk research only; no outreach sent; no reviewer has been recruited.  
**Last checked:** 2026-11-21  
**Purpose:** identify independent institutional and professional channels through which to recruit two reviewers for the role-blind review of 206 image observations.

## 1. Recruitment target

Recruit **two reviewers who independently assess all 206 supplied image observations** across the 32 original HEIG-VD images. The task is image-point suitability review, not re-running the original Metashape adjustment and not certifying survey accuracy.

Each reviewer should independently assess visibility, target identity, centring, blur, occlusion and ambiguity, using the prescribed decision vocabulary. The two submissions must remain separate until both are complete; disagreements then go to documented adjudication.

A candidate is not eligible merely because their institution teaches GIS or remote sensing. Confirm practical experience in aerial or close-range photogrammetry, image measurement, ground-control identification, photogrammetric QA, or equivalent geomatics practice.

## 3. Professional networks for circulation

These networks are better used to distribute the invitation or recommend qualified members than to treat membership as proof of competence.

- [ISPRS Student and Early Professionals (STEP)](https://sc.isprs.org/about/isprs_step/): international student and early-professional network in photogrammetry, remote sensing and geospatial information sciences. Public contact: step@isprs.org. Useful for identifying capable early-career researchers, but experience must be checked.
- [ISPRS Working Group I/2 — Mobile Mapping Technology](https://www2.isprs.org/commissions/comm1/wg2/): scope includes UAV/terrestrial sensors, close-range photogrammetry, image-based measurement and mapping. Approach only where an individual’s expertise matches the aerial-image review.
- [FIG Commissions](https://www.fig.net/organisation/comm//): Commission 5 (Positioning and Measurement) and Commission 6 (Engineering Surveys) are relevant professional channels for measurement and quality-control expertise. Ask for circulation or referrals; do not assume all members have photogrammetry experience.

## 4. Independence and conflict screen

Before sharing the full reviewer packet, each candidate must complete the existing disclosure/intake process. At minimum establish:

1. No participation in the HEIG-VD dataset’s acquisition, original Metashape measurements/adjustment, publication, or preparation of this rehearsal.
2. No access to the proposed control/checkpoint split or role mapping before completing the blinded review.
3. Whether they have previously accessed this dataset, its original Metashape project, observation file, OPK file, or any derived point annotations.
4. Any professional, institutional, financial or personal relationship with the dataset publisher, its authors, the project team or another reviewer.
5. Relevant practical experience, including the type of imagery, software/workflow, image measurement and QA work performed.
6. Ability to work from the original unannotated full-resolution images and preserve confidential reviewer submissions.

A prior exposure to the public dataset does not automatically disqualify a candidate, but exposure to the original measurements, OPK or point annotations compromises the intended independent image assessment and must be evaluated before acceptance. Anyone involved in the original processing is not suitable for this independent review.

Do not disclose the proposed control/checkpoint point IDs or split key during recruitment or review.

## 5. Recommended recruitment sequence

1. Start with KISM and DeKUT, while simultaneously approaching TU Dresden and NTUA. These offer the clearest combination of direct subject fit and institutional credibility.
2. Approach UoN and HFT Stuttgart as the next pair of channels.
3. Use ISPRS STEP and relevant ISPRS/FIG networks for broader circulation if the first wave does not produce two eligible reviewers.
4. Aim for two reviewers from different institutions and with no shared involvement in the source dataset.
5. Complete disclosure screening before issuing the role-blind packet and pinned-source acquisition instructions.
6. Give both reviewers the same materials, protocol, scope and deadline. Preserve separate original submissions.
7. Only after both submissions are complete should an adjudicator reconcile disagreements and prepare the final decision worksheet for the lock utility.

It does not ask the reviewer to validate survey accuracy or reproduce the original bundle adjustment. Two reviewers will work independently under a documented protocol, with conflict and prior-exposure disclosure before access. Would your department or network be willing to circulate the invitation to suitably experienced individuals?

No named person should be represented as a prospect or approached until their public profile and relevant experience have been individually checked.
