**Review date:** 18 November 2026

## Important non-equivalences and unresolved names

- **UrbanCLIP and UrbanVLP are separate models.** UrbanVLP's paper explicitly positions itself against shortcomings in text generated by the earlier UrbanCLIP approach and adds satellite plus street-view multi-granularity information.
- **UniST, UrbanGPT and UrbanDiT are distinct spatiotemporal model families.** Their papers, tasks and architectures differ; a shared urban-forecasting use case does not make them one model.
- **TrajFM and UniTraj are separate trajectory-model papers.** Do not merge their architecture, dataset or evaluation claims.
- **GeoGalactica, K2, GeoGPT and GeoLLM are not aliases.** In particular, GeoLLM is an ICLR 2024 method using LLM knowledge with OSM, while GeoGPT is a named geoscience model release/project.
- **GeoBenchX, K2's GeoBench, GeoAgentBench and GeoAnalystBench are separate benchmarks.** Benchmark names must be disambiguated by paper, task set and release.
- **OpenCity, STFM-style universal forecasters, GeoLLM-Engine, SpatialEval and GeoQA remain unresolved in this pass.** Do not infer identity from a similar acronym or product name.
- The broad time-series model names (TimesFM, Chronos, Moirai and Lag-Llama) are not audited here. They require separate paper/checkpoint/version/licence records and a clear explanation of what spatial adaptation adds.

## Status decision

The named items above can move from “identity not checked” to **identity located / bibliography lead recorded**. None should be marked fully verified. Before a canonical lesson or integration is considered complete, continue with versioned model cards, official code, dataset provenance, checkpoint access, licences, technical-claim review, task-specific baselines and independent reproduction.

The audit deliberately preserves the original Section 13 source grouping and its memory-based warning. It does not endorse model performance claims or grant permission to redistribute code, weights, data or figures.
