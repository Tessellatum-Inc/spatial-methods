# Licences and material scope

This repository uses more than one licence because software, learning material and source evidence have different reuse needs.

## Project licences

| Material | Licence | SPDX / canonical terms |
|---|---|---|
| Original source code, tests, scripts and executable examples | Apache License 2.0 | `Apache-2.0`; see [LICENSE](LICENSE) |
| Original explanatory prose, tutorials, exercises, notebooks' narrative text and project-created diagrams | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International | `CC-BY-4.0`; see [LICENSE-DOCUMENTATION](LICENSE-DOCUMENTATION) |

Executable code blocks intended to be copied and run are treated as code under Apache-2.0. The surrounding explanation and original diagrams are CC BY 4.0. Where a file contains mixed material, its front matter or adjacent notice should identify the applicable scope.

## Material not covered by the project-wide licences

The following are excluded unless an individual file or asset carries an explicit licence notice:

- the preserved page-by-page transcription in `source-inventory/pages-*.md`, pending page-level review of any incorporated third-party content;
- third-party papers, quotations, diagrams, photographs, maps, icons, software documentation and code samples;
- external datasets, model weights, benchmarks and linked resources;
- third-party packages and vendored code;
- trademarks, logos, names and branding.

The project owner has confirmed that the 47-page *Spatial Analysis Algorithms: Complete Compendium* is their own project and compilation, developed with AI assistance. That confirms project provenance; it does not automatically grant rights in third-party works incorporated into the source. See [compendium provenance](docs/compendium-provenance.md).

A citation is not a reproduction licence. If rights are unclear, link to the source instead of copying the asset. Maintain creator, source URL, exact licence/version, attribution, modifications and review date in the relevant register.

## Contributions

Contributions to original code are submitted under Apache-2.0; contributions to original documentation and diagrams are submitted under CC BY 4.0, subject to their respective scopes and the [Developer Certificate of Origin](DCO). Contributors retain copyright in their contributions and certify their right to submit them. Use `git commit -s` to add the required sign-off to each contribution commit.

Do not submit confidential, personal, restricted, unlicensed or otherwise unauthorised material. If a contribution includes third-party content, identify its origin and compatible licence before submission.

## Patreon and paid learning

Publicly licensed GitHub material remains public and reusable under its licence. Patreon payments do not add restrictions to that material and do not grant exclusive rights to it. Paid offerings may provide original guided curricula, annotated study plans, workshops, office hours, curated sequences, additional exercises and solutions, community support and other separately created services. Any paid asset derived from third-party material must independently comply with that material's terms.

## No blanket rights over external material

The licences above apply only to material the project has authority to license. They do not relicense dependencies, data, external references or third-party assets. See [licensing and reuse](docs/licensing-and-reuse.md) for the operational policy.
