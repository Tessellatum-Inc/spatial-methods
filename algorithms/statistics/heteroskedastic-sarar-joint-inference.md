# Joint inference for heteroskedastic SARAR GTSLS

## Model and estimator

For the common-weights SARAR(1,1) model,

\[
y=X\beta+\rho Wy+u,\qquad u=\lambda Wu+\varepsilon,
\]

the three-step estimator first obtains 2SLS residuals, estimates the spatial-error parameter \(\lambda\) from the two heteroskedastic Kelejian–Prucha (KP) quadratic moments, and applies the Cochrane–Orcutt transformation before a final 2SLS regression.

Point estimation alone does not provide valid joint uncertainty for the regression coefficients and \(\lambda\): the disturbance predictor is generated by the first-stage IV estimator, and the GM moments and final IV score are correlated.

## Joint covariance construction

The implementation follows Kelejian and Prucha (2010), Theorem 4 and Section 4.1, for \(M=W\), with identity weighting of the two GM moments.

Let \(Z=[X,Wy]\), \(H=[X,WX,\ldots,W^qX]\), and \(D=-Z\). Define the two zero-diagonal moment matrices

\[
A_1=W'W-\operatorname{diag}(W'W),\qquad A_2=W.
\]

With \(C=I-\hat\lambda W\), the first-stage IV map is

\[
\tilde P=(H'H)^{-1}H'Z[Z'H(H'H)^{-1}H'Z]^{-1},
\]

and the first-stage influence matrix is \(T=(I-\hat\lambda W')^{-1}H\tilde P\). The generated-residual correction vectors are estimated as

\[
\tilde a_r=T\,n^{-1}D'C'(A_r+A_r')C\tilde u,\quad r=1,2.
\]

Set \(\tilde\varepsilon=C\tilde u\), \(\tilde\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\tilde\varepsilon_i^2)\), and \(\tilde a=[\tilde a_1,\tilde a_2]\). The two-moment covariance is

\[
\tilde\Psi_{rs}=\frac{1}{2n}\operatorname{tr}[(A_r+A_r')\tilde\Sigma(A_s+A_s')\tilde\Sigma]
+\frac{1}{n}\tilde a_r'\tilde\Sigma\tilde a_s.
\]

For the final transformed IV estimator let \(Z_*=CZ\), and let \(P_*\) be its 2SLS influence map. Since the final estimator's influence matrix is \(H P_*\), the stacked moment covariance is

\[
\tilde\Psi_\circ=
\begin{bmatrix}
n^{-1}H'\tilde\Sigma H & n^{-1}H'\tilde\Sigma\tilde a\\
n^{-1}\tilde a'\tilde\Sigma H & \tilde\Psi
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

The identity-weighted GM derivative map is \(j'/(j'j)\), where \(j=[1,2\hat\lambda]'\). Applying the block map \(\operatorname{diag}(P_*',j'/(j'j))\) to \(\tilde\Psi_\circ\) gives the joint covariance of \([\hat\beta',\hat\rho,\hat\lambda]'\).

## Implementation and interpretation

- Runnable routine: `implementations/python/spatial-econometrics/spatial_sarar_joint_covariance.py`.
- Integrated output: `covariance_joint_kp` and `standard_errors_joint_kp` on the result of `spatial_sarar_gtsls`.
- Parameter order is **[beta..., rho, lambda]**. This differs from the order in some paper equations, so preserve the declared order when constructing Wald tests.
- The previous `covariance_hc1_conditional` remains available and is explicitly conditional on the estimated \(\lambda\); it is not interchangeable with the joint KP covariance.
- The implementation uses the identity weighting matrix for the GM moments, not the paper's optional efficient weighting based on \(\Psi^{-1}\).
- This is a plug-in asymptotic covariance under the paper's assumptions: independent innovations with suitable moments, bounded spatial operators/instruments, valid instruments, and identified moment and IV systems. It is not a finite-sample exact covariance or a spatial HAC estimator.
- The code is dense-matrix teaching software and is restricted to \(M=W\).

## Reference
