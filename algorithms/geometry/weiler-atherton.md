# Weiler–Atherton polygon intersection\n\nWeiler–Atherton represents subject and clipping boundaries as linked cycles, inserts paired intersection events in edge order, classifies entry/exit events and traverses alternating boundary arcs to assemble intersection components. The implementation supports simple concave rings and disconnected intersections, but not holes, vertex-touch or collinear-overlap events.\n\nSee [implementation and tests](../../implementations/python/weiler-atherton/README.md).
